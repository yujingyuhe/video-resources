# 版本记录 · Changelog

## 1.5

- 默认库之外最多可添加 10 个独立视频库，可自定义名称，并从左侧切换。
- 横屏、竖屏和方形缩略图按视频比例自动排列。
- 扩展常见视频格式的播放兼容性，需要时生成本地播放缓存。
- 放大播放器加入上一条和下一条视频按钮。

- Add and name up to 10 libraries alongside the default library and switch between them from the sidebar.
- Browse landscape, portrait and square thumbnails at their video aspect ratios.
- Play more common formats with a local compatibility cache when needed.
- Open the previous or next video from the expanded player.

- First Microsoft Store release with Chinese and English interfaces, category and color filters, ratings, video metadata, thumbnail scrubbing and frame-by-frame viewing.
